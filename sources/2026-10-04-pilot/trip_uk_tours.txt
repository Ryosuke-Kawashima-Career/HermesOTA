Trip.com Group and Women in Travel CIC Launch Three New Women-Led Tours Across the UK
Oct 02, 2026
Partnerships
Trip.com

Trip.com Group, a leading global travel service provider, and social enterprise Women in Travel CIC have launched three new women-led tours in London, Edinburgh and Manchester, created by graduates of the Enterprising Tour Guiding Academy.

Building on a collaboration launched in 2024, the latest phase of the partnership is helping Academy graduates turn their knowledge, experiences and local perspectives into bookable tourism businesses. Women in Travel CIC provides participants with training and practical support to develop their tours, while Trip.com Group is supporting graduates in bringing those experiences to market and reaching travellers through Trip.com, the Group’s all-in-one travel app.



The project aims to create new routes into the travel industry for women who may face barriers to entry, including those from underserved or underrepresented backgrounds. Through training and practical support, participants can develop their own tourism experiences, generate income, and share authentic stories about the communities they know best.

The Enterprising Tour Guiding Academy equips participants with the practical and commercial skills needed to create and deliver high-quality tours for domestic and international travellers. By supporting women to establish their own tourism businesses, the programme encourages micro-entrepreneurship while bringing new voices and perspectives to the visitor experience. The three new experiences span food, history, music and culture, with each reflecting the knowledge and perspective of the woman who created it.


The new tours include:

• Imperial feast on a Plate - London, a food-focused walking tour exploring 2,000 years of migration through London’s culinary heritage, from Roman influences to South Asian and Caribbean communities.
• Gothic Edinburgh - an immersive walking experience uncovering the Scottish capital’s darker history through Gothic architecture, stories of women and a Victorian-inspired afternoon tea.
• Beyond the Bassline - Manchester, a music and culture tour examining how migration, identity and diverse communities helped shape Manchester’s world-famous music scene.



Jane McFadzean, Senior Director of Global Sustainability at Trip.com Group, said: “Through our collaboration with Women in Travel CIC’s Enterprising Tour Guiding Academy, we are supporting women to turn their knowledge and ideas into tourism businesses that can generate income and reach travellers from around the world. By helping graduates bring their experiences to market through Trip.com, we hope to create practical opportunities for entrepreneurship while bringing new voices and perspectives to the travel industry.”

Alessandra Alonso, Founder and Managing Director at Women in Travel CIC says: “The Enterprising Tour Guiding Academy was created to give women not only the skills and confidence to build careers in tourism, but a practical route to turn their knowledge and ideas into viable businesses. Seeing Martina, Pola and Saj take their individual concepts from the Academy through to fully bookable experiences is exactly what we want the programme to enable.

“Our collaboration with Trip.com Group gives these women the opportunity to reach travellers beyond their own networks and build greater visibility for their businesses. We’re incredibly proud to see their perspectives and stories becoming part of the visitor experience in London, Edinburgh and Manchester.”



The tours are now available to book via Trip.com here.

Trip.com is dedicated to creating employment opportunities for underrepresented women around the world. The collaboration forms part of Trip.com Group’s broader work to support women’s employment and entrepreneurship across the tourism industry.
