QANTAS LOYALTY PARTNERS WITH EXPEDIA GROUP TO TRANSFORM QANTAS HOTELS & HOLIDAYS BOOKING EXPERIENCE
September 30, 2026
Download
PDF Format (opens in new window)

September 2026:
Qantas Loyalty has today announced a strategic technology partnership with Expedia Group, set to transform the digital booking experience and technology capabilities across the Qantas Hotels & Holidays and Jetstar Holidays platforms.
Under the agreement, Expedia Group B2B will power Qantas’ end-to-end travel experience designed specifically for the Qantas Hotels and Holidays ecosystem. Built on Expedia Group B2B’s Composable Agentic Template, customers will have a single place to book hotels and holiday packages, earn and redeem Qantas Points, and manage their entire trip.
The solution will deliver a faster, smarter, and more intuitive booking experience while unlocking new growth opportunities for hotel suppliers. New benefits will include:
Deeper integration into the Qantas App and improved digital self-servicing capabilities
Machine learning-powered property and holiday recommendations
The ability to bundle holiday package bookings with premium cabin seating and partner codeshare flights
Access to Expedia Group B2B’s technology to unlock ongoing new releases, ensuring Qantas Hotels & Holidays is evolving with the market
First-of-its-kind capabilities providing Qantas and its hotel suppliers with more seamless connectivity options
Qantas Loyalty Executive Manager, Business to Consumer, Jayne Davey said: “Qantas Hotels and Holidays continues to play a key role within the Qantas Group ecosystem, allowing millions of customers to connect their flights, stays and travel experiences into one seamless journey.
“Partnering with Expedia Group gives us access to world-class travel technology that will transform how our customers search, book, and manage their trips. This solution not only allows for deeper customisation and smarter search tools, but it also provides the foundation we need to continuously innovate, deploy new features, and support our long-term growth strategy.”
Carolina Cabero, SVP Sales, Expedia Group B2B said: “Trust is the foundation of every successful partnership, and our ongoing relationship with Qantas reflects the power of building together for the long term.
“We’re proud to create a B2B platform that can be fully customized and brings together Expedia Group B2B’s world-class travel technology with Qantas’ deep understanding of its members and partners. By connecting supply, pricing, payments, loyalty and smarter digital experiences through one integrated platform, we’re giving Qantas the flexibility to innovate faster, deliver more personalised journeys and continue evolving Hotels & Holidays for the future.”
Build and integration work between Qantas Loyalty and Expedia Group B2B is currently underway, with the upgraded Hotels and Holidays platform scheduled to launch in H1 2027.
