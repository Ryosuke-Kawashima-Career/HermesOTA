What is Lola?

What is Lola?

Lola is your insider across travel, dining, and everything worth doing. You tell Lola what you are looking for in your own words, and Lola comes back with places/events chosen for you, live prices and availability, and a way to book inside the conversation.

02

02

How do I get access?

How do I get access?

Join at lola.com. Lola is opening to members in waves, and invitations generally go out in the order people join. Lola becomes generally available later this fall.

03

03

Do I need an account?

Do I need an account?

Yes. You give your name, email and a few details that help Lola make recommendations for you.

04

04

Is Lola free?

Is Lola free?

Yes. Lola membership is free and gives you conversational discovery, planning and booking, with real-time availability, pricing verified by us and/or our partners and recommendations that get more useful the more you use Lola.

05

05

Does Lola have paid plans?

Does Lola have paid plans?

Yes. Lola Plus and VIP are coming this fall, with more rates, access and benefits on top of everything in the free tier. Pricing and benefits will be made available when the plans open.

06

06

Can I plan and book hotels, restaurants and activities in the same conversation? What about flights?

Can I plan and book hotels, restaurants and activities in the same conversation? What about flights?

Yes, you can book hotels, restaurants, and activities one at a time or all together as part of a plan. There are some restaurants and activities that you may need to book separately from Lola. Lola will advise you. Currently Lola does not yet cover flights, that is coming soon.

07

How is Lola different from asking a general AI assistant to plan a trip?

How is Lola different from asking a general AI assistant to plan a trip?

Lola is connected to live inventory, so the hotel, table or ticket you see is real, current and bookable in that moment. Lola provides a selection of booking sources for hotel stays so that you can compare prices yourself. Lola also carries what it learns forward. Tell Lola once that you sleep badly near elevators, and it applies that on the next trip and the one after.

08

08

How is Lola different from a travel booking site?

How is Lola different from a travel booking site?

Booking sites search one category at a time and leave you to assemble the trip. Lola works across hotels, restaurants and activities in a single conversation, keeps track of the plan, and books the pieces as you decide on them. In addition to a free membership, Lola also offers two paid membership plans that provide insider benefits across hotels, dining and experiences.
