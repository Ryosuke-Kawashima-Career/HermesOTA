# Hermes AI Agent

This AI agents fetches news article, financial data and UI/UX change of OTA sites such as Expedia, Agoda, Booking.com, Kiwi.com, Etraveli, Yeogiotte, etc. The scope is the OTA all over the world. The AI agent reports prominent strategy and AI usage of the rival OTAs based on the revenue and prospect in the future. The main purpose of this system is to devise strong long-term strategies to win the market of traveling.
